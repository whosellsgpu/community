## Why we exist

Finding GPU capacity is still mostly word of mouth. Teams waste weeks chasing quotes, and providers with capacity struggle to reach the teams that need it.

This community is an open place to close that gap: ask for compute, offer compute, and share what the market really looks like.

## What you can do here

- **Request GPUs.** Tell people what you need, and let providers come to you.
- **List supply.** Share capacity you can offer, whether it's a few cards or a full cluster.
- **Talk pricing and the market.** Compare notes on rates, availability, contracts and hardware.

## A good post includes

- **GPU model and count**, for example 64× H100 SXM
- **Duration**, such as on-demand, 3 months or 1 year
- **Region**, plus any data-residency needs
- **Timeline**: when you need it, or when it's available
- **Budget or price**, if you can share it

## Guidelines

1. **Be specific.** Clear numbers get real replies.
2. **Be honest.** Only list capacity you can actually deliver, at prices you'll actually honour.
3. **One post per need.** Update your post instead of reposting, and close it once it's filled.
4. **No spam.** No cold sales pitches, unrelated promotion or referral links.
5. **Be respectful.** Disagree about ideas, never about people.
6. **Keep private details private.** Move contracts, credentials and payment talk to direct contact.
7. **Do your own diligence.** Who Sells GPU doesn't verify listings or take part in deals.

See something that breaks these rules? Use **Report** on the post.
